SUPPORT AT HOME

Home-care services

Questions to ask when arranging care at home.

Choosing a care provider

  • Confirm caregiver identity, qualifications, and references.
  • Discuss the schedule, responsibilities, and fees in advance.
  • Agree on emergency contacts and family updates.
  • Ask whether the service can meet mobility or personal-care needs.

CARE OPTIONS

Old-age home directory

Use this checklist when comparing residential care options.

What to check before choosing

  • Staff availability and nursing support, including overnight.
  • Cleanliness, meals, accessibility, and activities.
  • Visiting hours, costs, services, and admission requirements.
  • Visit in person and ask about resident safety and complaint procedures.

A live directory and availability checker will be added in a later version.

MIND & CONNECTION

Emotional well-being

Little moments of connection can make a difference.

Ideas for today

  • Call or message someone you enjoy talking to.
  • Spend time on a favourite hobby, music, or a gentle activity.
  • Plan a regular visit or shared meal with someone you trust.
  • If sadness or loneliness continues, consider talking with a qualified professional.
